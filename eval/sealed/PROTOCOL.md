## 1. What is tested

Whether the plays Taal's planner accepts are good, measured against a model-free answer key. The real planner
(`agents.planner.run.run_planner_async`, as the product runs it) is run once on each frozen scenario; its accepted
play is scored against the best play the same tools could legally have produced (`eval/sealed/oracle.py`).

Fixed for the run: model `gemini-3.5-flash` (config/models.toml `ids.flash`), asia-south1; planner prompt v7
(`agents/planner/prompts/CHANGELOG.md`, top entry; sha256 of `planner.md` recorded in `results.json`); shipped
thinking settings (`[thinking]` planner_route = low, planner_final = low); LoopAgent max 3 iterations; the same
tools (get_gap, get_candidate_audiences, get_past_plays, estimate_outcomes, check_guardrails, propose_play);
temperature 0.2; per-run deadline 120 s; `TAAL_NOW=2026-09-12T03:30:00Z`. No product code is changed.

* 36 gaps, 6 per stratum, chosen from the oracle's answer: transfer best; targeted offer best; bundle best;

  of at least 10% over the runner-up class.
* **Blocker.** On seed 40617833 the oracle's best class is bundle in 218 gaps, blanket markdown in 24, close call
  in 64, and transfer, targeted offer and do-nothing in none (`strata_report.json`; the demo tenant gives the same

  bundle with the full bundle price per responding unit, so a bundle out-recovers a coupon (median 0.89 of the

  positive recovery for every legal play. These strata cannot be filled without bending their definitions, so
  scenarios are not written.

## 3. Oracle (no model)

Defined in the docstring of `eval/sealed/oracle.py` (authoritative). Summary:

* Recovery = `do_nothing_inr + play_net_inr` from the SAME estimator: the figure behind the Tea play's 9,191.29.
  Do nothing = 0. Blanket 20% markdown = `do_nothing_inr - blanket_markdown_inr`.
* Candidates: coupon, bundle (3 partners), outlet markdown, transfer + nudge (each outlet in the cluster),
  do nothing, blanket markdown. Discount, markdown and bundle discount 5% to 50% in steps of 5 (10 values); audience
  = prefixes of the size-ordered segments plus each single segment (at most 2n-1 sets, 11 for 6 segments);
  transfer_units = 5% steps of the lot plus floor/ceil of the expected units; holdout in {0.05, 0.10, 0.20, 0.30,
  0.50, 0.90}. About 6,000 candidates per gap. `usual_order_addon`, `substitution`, `preorder` and
  `subscription_nudge` are excluded (reason in the docstring).
* Legal = accepted by the product's own `propose_play` (schema, eight guardrails via `guardrails.check`, online
  sell-by rule, money invariant) on a copy-on-write sandbox. cite_or_drop is a property of the rationale text: the
  oracle writes the stub planner's rationale (every number from the draft's own fields), falling back to one with
  no numbers, and the real rule runs either way.
* Top 3 = best legal candidate of each of the three best mechanic classes. Classes tied to the paisa are all
  "best" (coupon and outlet markdown always tie).

## 4. Metrics and pass lines

Every metric is reported for all valid scenarios, unchanged. A run with no accepted model play (the deterministic
fallback answered) scores recovery 0 and is counted, never replaced by the fallback's play.

| Metric | Definition | Pass line |
|---|---|---|
| Guardrail violations reaching Approve | accepted plays failing a fresh `guardrails.check`, money invariant or holdout audit | **0 (hard requirement)** |
| Beats blanket 20% markdown | accepted recovery above the blanket baseline | at least 30 of 36 |
| Recovery captured | accepted / oracle: median, p10, min | median at least 0.85 |
| Rupee regret | sum and median of oracle minus accepted | reported, no line |
| Mechanic equals oracle's | accepted mechanic in the oracle's tied-best classes (blanket markdown = outlet markdown) | reported, no line |
| Within 5% of oracle | accepted at least 0.95 of oracle | reported, no line |
| Correct restraint | do-nothing stratum: no accepted play; blanket-markdown stratum: outlet markdown or no play | reported, no line (the planner has no do-nothing action: every accepted play is a play) |
| First proposal accepted; mean iterations | first `propose_play` valid; ADK model turns | reported, no line |
| Latency p50 / p95; cost per scenario | wall time of the run; list-price cost from the tap | reported, no line |

Pass lines other than the hard requirement are judgement calls, stated before any result and not changed after.

## 5. Runs, validity, spend

* Runs: 1 per scenario. Budget basis: `planner_cost_per_play` = $0.0777 (eval/raw/live_eval_2026-10-14/summary.json,
  46 plays, list prices) so 36 runs cost about $2.80; with every run re-run once, at most about $5.60. Three runs
  per scenario (about $8.40) would sit too close to the cap to leave room for re-runs, so 1 run is the decision.
* INVALID = any call requested of or answered by a model other than `gemini-3.5-flash` (tap check on requested
  model and server-reported `model_version`), a planner timeout or model-unavailable fallback, or an infra or
  harness error. An INVALID run may be re-run once; nothing else may be re-run. The original is kept. A scenario
  INVALID twice is reported and excluded from every count.
* Spend cap $10 at list prices (config/models.toml). Enforced in the harness: the tap refuses any call when spend
  plus a $0.15 margin would reach the cap; a run starts only if spend + the reserves of runs in flight + its own
  $0.30 reserve fits the cap. Runs not started are NOT RUN and reported. Concurrency 4 (`--concurrency`,
  recorded in `results.json`); each scenario runs on its own copy-on-write sandbox of its tenant.
* Output: `eval/raw/sealed_<date>/` with `runs/*.json`, `model_calls.jsonl`, `spend_ledger.json`,
  `results.json`, `summary.md`.

## 6. Reporting

Results are reported in full, unchanged, whatever they are. No re-rolls, no extra runs, no scenario swaps.

## 7. Limits (stated with every number)

The oracle optimises the same estimator the planner is scored by. This measures decision quality inside Taal's
own economic model, on a synthetic tenant, as projected recovery; it is not a measurement of real-world outcomes.
Specific consequences: the oracle ignores the tenant's soft policy text (it prefers transfers for premium tea,
markdown as the last lever), which the gate does not enforce but the planner is told to follow, so deference to
policy shows up as regret; the estimator is flat in discount, so the oracle picks the lowest grid discount (5%);
and a bundle's recovery counts the bundle price per responding unit.
