On 12 Oct the 46 evalsets were 28 Sep recordings from `gemini-2.5-flash` (the 46 evalsets in the repo were re-recorded on 14 Oct from `gemini-3.5-flash`, prompt v7, `agents/planner/evalsets/PROVENANCE.json`; the 28 Sep set is at `git show 6482e7c:agents/planner/evalsets/<file>`); 41 of the 45 trajectory failures expect two or more `propose_play` attempts, while `gemini-3.5-flash` proposed first time in 40 of those 45, so the strict score measures departure from the old retry pattern, not planner quality. Post-hoc measure: the first proposed play passed the gate in 41 of 46 evalsets on `gemini-3.5-flash` (12 Oct, prompt v6) vs 5 of 46 on `gemini-2.5-flash` (9 Oct, pre-switch); this is the 40 of the 45 trajectory failures that proposed first time plus the one exact-trajectory match. Exact tool trajectory stays 1/46 (`gemini-3.5-flash`, prompt v6, 12 Oct; 6/46 on 9 Oct, `gemini-2.5-flash`, pre-switch). Each retry is a play the gate refused (schema, guardrails or money checks); in the recordings themselves (28 Sep, `gemini-2.5-flash`, pre-switch) the first play passed in 2/46, and `check_guardrails` ran before the proposal in 46/46 (`gemini-3.5-flash`, 12 Oct; 23/46 on 9 Oct, `gemini-2.5-flash`, pre-switch) (`eval/raw/live_eval_2026-10-12/first_attempt.json`, `harness/first_attempt_acceptance.py`). The gate changed between the two runs (it now re-derives audience and estimate), so the five fully recorded `gemini-2.5-flash` runs were replayed through today's gate: it still rejects all five first attempts. Five runs is a small check, not proof. Also computed from the stored results: in all 46 cases it called every expected tool and ended with `propose_play`; IN_ORDER and ANY_ORDER matching are both 5/46, for the same retry reason (a different measure from the first-proposal counts above). The set is a regression guard against recorded behaviour, not an accuracy score, and it was re-recorded on 14 Oct (see above). The 3 response misses are recordings that ended in an empty final turn; the model returned a play in all three.
