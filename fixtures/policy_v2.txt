Kutumb Mart pricing and promotion policy.
1. Never sell below the category margin floor.
2. Prefer bundles and transfers over blanket markdowns; markdowns are the last lever.
3. Never discount premium tea below 12% margin.
4. Any near-sell-by offer must state the best-before date to the customer.
5. Customers with an active subscription for a product never receive discount offers on it.
6. Do not push a product that is at risk of stockout at the same node.
7. Every play must carry a holdout of at least 5% so we can measure it.
